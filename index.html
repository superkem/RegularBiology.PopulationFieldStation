      Case file: A plague of rabbits

      In 1859, 24 rabbits were released in Australia. Collect the clues, then check your answers.

      Clue 1: The shape of the first years

      Australia had almost no predators, parasites or diseases for rabbits. What kind of curve did the population make at first?

      Which reason fits best?

      Clue 2: England and Australia

      Rabbits in England never exploded the way they did in Australia. Select every factor that was present for English rabbits but missing in Australia.

      Clue 3: Locked

      Unlocks in Lesson 5.3, Human Population Growth. Australians eventually released a disease on purpose. Which type of limiting factor is that?

      Exit ticket

      Explain why exponential growth cannot continue forever, and why a rabbit population escapes its limits faster than an elephant population would.
